    Device and help
    
  
    
    
    
    
    Pairing: the first connection asks for the 6-digit passkey set in CC1101_Workbench/config.h.
      If pairing fails after re-flashing, open iPad Settings › Bluetooth, forget “RF Workbench”, and connect again.
    
    
    Transmit timing resolution is 1 µs; this is not a calibrated accuracy claim. Timing consistency measures repeated
      pulse structure, not decoder success. Module-rated transmit range: 387–464 MHz; other chip bands are experimental receive-only.
      Replaying a rolling-code remote is normally rejected by its receiver. Use with your own devices, or with permission.
    
    
    Auto Catch runs in this page: keep the iPad awake and this tab in front while it watches. Signals are stored in this
      browser; use Export all for a backup.
